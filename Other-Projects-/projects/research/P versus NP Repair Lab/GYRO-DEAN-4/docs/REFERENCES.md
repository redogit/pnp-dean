# External References

These references support standard background facts used by the project. Project theorem files are written to be as self-contained as practical.

- Clay Mathematics Institute, **P vs NP** (official status and 400-student / 100-dorm example):
  https://www.claymath.org/millennium/p-vs-np/

- Fractional Vertex Cover half-integrality and Nemhauser-Trotter background (Algorithmica exposition):
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00453-012-9660-4

- Roger E. Behrend, **Fractional Perfect b-Matching Polytopes. I: General Theory** (extreme fractional perfect matching structure):
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.7356

- Michel Goemans, **Matroid Optimization lecture notes** (rank-function submodularity):
  https://math.mit.edu/~goemans/18433S09/matroid-notes.pdf

- Grosu, Person, Szabo, **On the rank of higher inclusion matrices** (Gottlieb full-rank theorem background):
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2197

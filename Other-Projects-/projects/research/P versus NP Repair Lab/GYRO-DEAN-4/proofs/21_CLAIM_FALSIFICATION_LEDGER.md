## False Claim D — "Original-variable maximal PCA always reaches logarithmic affine dimension"

Status: FALSE. PCA must be one gyroscopic carrier, not the universal terminal measure.

## False Claim E — "Unary/pairwise semantic facts must expose progress"

The Petersen graph at q=4 has five maximum independent sets whose incidences make every vertex nonforced and realize all graph-permitted pair patterns. Hence there is no new unary/pairwise no-good beyond the original edges, although stronger carriers solve the graph.

Status: FALSE as a universal basis-progress theorem.

## False Claim F — "Just increase polynomial lift degree until the problem is easy"

Even the empty graph fixed-q slice has degree-k evaluation rank governed by large inclusion matrices; algebraic completion can require large degree while the graph carrier solves immediately. A static lift-to-completion policy is therefore not an efficient universal strategy.

Status: FALSE as an algorithmic policy.

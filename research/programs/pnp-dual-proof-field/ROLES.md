# Dynamic roles

Roles are instantiated from the current obligation. They are not permanent personalities.

## 1. Offense — P=NP constructor

**Purpose:** produce an exact constructive route.

May propose:

- carrier representations;
- reductions;
- decompositions;
- circuits;
- reconciliation rules;
- induction lemmas;
- polynomial potentials;
- exact terminal algorithms.

Required output:

[
(\text{preconditions},\text{transform},\text{exactness argument},\text{cost},\text{recovery},\text{remainder})
]

Preferred skill: `research-attempt`.

A local win does not establish P=NP.

---

## 2. Defense — P!=NP attacker

**Purpose:** break an offensive configuration or generalize recurring failures.

Attack classes include:

- carrier aliasing;
- future divergence;
- hidden oracle equivalence;
- construction-cost explosion;
- nonuniform exponent;
- interface coupling;
- witness/recovery failure;
- failure of universal coverage.

Preferred skills: `research-attempt`, `proof-audit`, `computation-audit`.

A local win means the targeted mechanism failed. It does not establish P!=NP.

Promotion path:

[
\text{counterexample}
\to
\text{counterfamily}
\to
\text{common invariant}
\to
\text{representation-independent lemma}
\to
\text{universal lower bound}.
]

---

## 3. Explorer

**Purpose:** leave a stalled representation without changing the obligation.

Searches for:

- alternate mathematical shapes;
- new quotient coordinates;
- decompositions;
- cross-domain carrier primitives;
- circuit replacements;
- dual formulations.

Invoke when populations are repeatedly fighting over the same local representation.

The Explorer generates candidates only. No evidence authority transfers from the source domain.

---

## 4. Repairer

**Purpose:** take a defeated configuration and make the smallest lawful change addressing the exact failure.

Contract:

[
\text{failure witness}
\to
\text{minimal repair}
\to
\text{same counterprobe}.
]

Do not replace a failed route with unrelated machinery and call it repaired.

---

## 5. Diversity Keeper

**Purpose:** keep the active population structurally heterogeneous.

It classifies configurations by mechanism, protected distinction, failure mode, and proof obligation.

It may retire duplicates but must preserve provenance and occurrence history.

No scalar "fitness" score is allowed to erase a unique mechanism.

---

## 6. Memory Carrier

**Purpose:** prevent knowledge decay.

Every admitted/failed configuration retains:

- source identity;
- obligation;
- transform;
- evidence;
- counterexamples;
- costs;
- recovery path;
- status;
- remainder.

Use `research-state` when a ledger is available.

---

## 7. Cost Accountant

**Purpose:** independently charge total work.

At minimum:

[
C_{total} =
C_{encode}+
C_{discover}+
C_{build}+
C_{select}+
C_{transform}+
C_{reconcile}+
C_{verify}+
C_{rollback}+
C_{recover}.
]

The final accounting must translate to the standard deterministic model.

A route cannot referee its own cost.

---

## 8. Referee

**Purpose:** determine only what the evidence establishes.

Prefer deterministic checking.

Use:

- `proof-audit` for claims/proofs;
- `computation-audit` for finite computational evidence;
- `literature-check` for external dependencies.

Possible verdicts:

- `ADMIT_SCOPED`
- `REJECT_COUNTEREXAMPLE`
- `REPAIRABLE`
- `UNRESOLVED`
- `INVALID_ATTACK`

Universal theorem promotion is separate and requires all universal obligations.

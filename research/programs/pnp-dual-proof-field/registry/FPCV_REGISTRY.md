## Registry interpretation

- `CORE` means central to the current obligation, not proved universal success.
- `TERMINAL` means an exact declared restricted class is closed.
- `BARRIER` preserves a failure/counterexample so it is not rediscovered or silently promoted.
- `REPRESENTATION` means the carrier/quotient is exact in its stated sense; value evaluation may remain open.
- `SESSION_DERIVATION_UNPROMOTED` entries are intentionally below repository theorem authority until reviewed.

Chronology is stored on each JSON entry. `semantic_ancestry` records conceptual dependence separately from date order.
